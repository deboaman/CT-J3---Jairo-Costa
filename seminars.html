About the instructor

Experience built on the mats.

Jairo Costa is a black belt instructor, athlete and coach. His work combines technical development, athlete education and a belief in Jiu-Jitsu as a tool for personal growth and social inclusion.

CT-3J reports that Jairo has helped develop more than 30 medal-winning athletes. Titles, dates, divisions and career details can be added once records are confirmed.

To invite Jairo

Include the essentials in your first message.

  • City, country and venue.
